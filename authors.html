For Authors

The journal welcomes submissions from students and emerging scholars working in literature, literary studies, culture, and related fields in the humanities.

We accept both research papers subject to editorial review and works in other literary and critical genres.

Sections

Research

Research articles are analytical works addressing a specific problem in literary studies, literary history, cultural studies, or a related field.

A research article should contain:

  • a clearly formulated research question or problem;
  • a central argument or thesis that the author seeks to substantiate;
  • a defined body of material, such as a literary work, a corpus of texts, a literary phenomenon, or historical material;
  • coherent and well-supported argumentation;
  • engagement with primary sources and, where appropriate, relevant scholarly literature;
  • an independent analytical contribution;
  • accurate citations and a bibliography.

An independent contribution may take the form of a new interpretation of a literary work, a previously unexplored comparison of texts or phenomena, the application of an established approach to new material, a refinement of an existing interpretation, a reasoned critique of a scholarly position, or another original observation.

All research articles undergo editorial peer review.

In evaluating a research article, the editors consider:

  • clarity of the research problem;
  • coherence and validity of the central argument;
  • quality of textual or other analysis;
  • use of primary sources and scholarly literature;
  • originality and independence of the research;
  • appropriateness of the chosen method;
  • logical structure;
  • quality of the scholarly apparatus;
  • clarity and precision of expression.

Following review, a submission may be accepted, accepted subject to minor revisions, returned for substantial revision, or rejected.

Essays

The journal welcomes literary, critical, historical, and cultural essays.

Essays are not required to follow the formal conventions of a research article and do not necessarily require a scholarly apparatus. However, they should present an independent line of thought, possess an internal coherence, and offer a meaningful perspective on their subject.

Reviews

The journal accepts reviews of works of literature, scholarly studies, collections, journals, and other significant literary or cultural publications.

A review should do more than summarize its subject: it should provide a critical reading and a reasoned assessment of the work.

Creative Writing

The journal accepts original literary works, including short stories, poetry, novellas, narrative poems, and other literary forms.

The editors consider the independence of the author's conception, artistic coherence, language, composition, and expressive quality.

Materials

This section may include translations, interviews, archival materials, letters, documentary and bibliographical materials, editions of previously unpublished or little-known texts, and other materials of literary or cultural interest.

For materials based on archival or other documentary sources, authors should provide information about the provenance of the material and, where possible, precise bibliographical or archival references.

General Requirements

  • Submissions should be original works that have not previously been published elsewhere, unless otherwise agreed with the editorial board.
  • Authors are responsible for the accuracy of factual statements, quotations, and bibliographical information, as well as for the proper use and attribution of external materials.
  • Plagiarism, deliberate fabrication of sources, invented quotations, and other forms of academic or intellectual misconduct constitute grounds for rejection.
  • The editors may ask authors to revise a submission before publication. Revisions may concern the content, structure, argumentation, language, citation practices, formatting, or other aspects of the work.
  • By submitting a work to the journal, the author confirms that they are its author and agrees to its consideration by the editorial board.

Research articles are subject to a separate peer-review procedure. All other submissions undergo editorial selection appropriate to their respective genres.
